Scope: this is the owner's authoritative finance database. Categories are tenant-local. The schema context includes the reporting currency selected when the conversation starts. Drafts are visible but ordinary analysis excludes financial_transaction.is_draft = 1.

Money: amount and currency_rate.rate are canonical decimal TEXT. SQLite SUM, AVG, TOTAL, CAST and arithmetic can use floating point: tool monetary results are approximate, not exact totals. Canonical stored money and the application's exact converter are unchanged. Do not perform unverified mental arithmetic or combine currencies without explicit valuation.

Currency conversion: currency_rate.day joins financial_transaction.transaction_date and currency_rate.currency joins the currency being valued. Rates are units per EUR; EUR has implicit rate 1. For source S and target T, use amount * CAST(target_rate AS REAL) / CAST(source_rate AS REAL), selecting each non-EUR rate on that transaction date. Source equal to target needs no rates. Use LEFT JOINs and report missing-rate records rather than silently losing them or treating missing rates as zero. Do not substitute today's rates. This is ECB reference valuation, not actual bank execution or fees; is_provisional identifies provisional stored rates.

Meaning: direction is Credit or Debit (cash direction), not income/spending classification. meaning is expense, income, refund, own_account_transfer, credit_card_payment or unknown. Transfers and card payments are excluded from both income and spending; refunds reduce the corresponding purchase category. Unknown meanings remain unresolved.

Recurrence is independent of meaning: recurring_bill, subscription, other_recurring, non_recurring or unknown. A recurring bill is not automatically a subscription; use stored classifications rather than inventing certainty from merchant names.

Dates: transaction_date is the transaction's original date. statement.statement_date may be NULL; the importer preserves an explicit date or uses the earliest valid transaction date when absent. Do not invent dates from filenames or row order.
